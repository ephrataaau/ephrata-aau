Fall AAU
Basketball

Boys and girls in grades 3-6 can sign up to play for Ephrata in the Moses Lake AAU league. Eight games over four Saturdays between Oct. 17 and Nov. 21. Sign up by Oct. 2.

The details

Who
Grades 3-6
Boys and girls. No tryouts. EYB does its best to find a team for every player who signs up.
When
Oct. 17-Nov. 21
Each team plays eight games on four Saturdays during that stretch. Your coach will share the schedule.
Where
Around the Basin
Moses Lake AAU league games are played in Moses Lake, Quincy, Warden and Royal City.
Cost
About $75
$22 AAU membership, paid to AAU, plus your share of the $450 team league fee, collected by your coach. Jerseys are an extra cost, coordinated by your coach.

What the $75 covers

Every player needs their own AAU membership for insurance and league play. You buy the membership directly from AAU. New AAU players should wait until they're placed on a team. The league charges $450 per team, split evenly among the players. Depending on team size, that works out to about $45-55 each. Your coach collects it. Jerseys cost extra, and your coach will coordinate them with your team.

What your player needs

Required for AAU
Court-only shoes

Every player needs a pair of shoes worn only inside the gym, for all games and practices. They don't have to be special basketball shoes. Players should arrive in their street shoes and change into their court-only shoes at the gym.

This helps our school district take care of its gym floors, and our use of the gyms depends on following this rule. If your child needs help getting a pair of court-only shoes, please let us know.

Each player needs their own ball, sized by grade.

How to sign up

1
Register
Fill out the online form by Oct. 2. It takes a few minutes.
2
We build the teams
EYB does its best to find a team for every player who signs up. Teams depend on how many kids register in each grade and on having a volunteer coach for each team.
3
We contact you
EYB sends next steps for buying your player's $22 AAU membership and puts you in touch with your coach. If your player is new to AAU or hasn't played AAU before, please wait until they're placed on a team before buying a membership.
4
Your coach takes it from there
Your coach collects each player's share of the $450 team league fee and registers the team with the league.
